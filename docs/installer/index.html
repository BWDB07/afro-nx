AFRO-nx Project on GitHub

Switch installer

Make an SD card ZIP from your Afro Samurai disc image. Your browser generates the Switch shaders from your ISO. Your disc files stay on this device.

Choose your disc image

Copy to your Switch

Save AFRO-nx.zip on your computer, then extract it to the root of your SD card. It includes the NRO, both generated shader files, the 30 FPS config, and game_root/ under switch/AFRO-nx/.

Use desktop Chrome or Edge with about 4 GB of free disk space. Shader compilation can take a few minutes.

AFRO-nx is maintained by BWDB07.
