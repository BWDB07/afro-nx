Disc extractor

Turn your Afro Samurai Xbox 360 disc image into an SD card ZIP. Your files stay on this device.

Choose your disc image

Copy to your Switch

Extract AFRO-nx-game-files.zip to the root of your SD card. The ZIP creates switch/AFRO-nx/game_root/. Use the build guide for the NRO and shaders. Save the ZIP on your computer first.

Use desktop Chrome or Edge with about 4 GB of free disk space.
