# AFRO-nx v0.1.0

- Original game timing with a 30 FPS cap at stock clocks.
- Native 720p profile with 2× MSAA.
- Known visual issue: one side of Afro's bandana can render dark during the prologue.
- The experimental 60 FPS mode is disabled. The encounter reported blocked at 60 FPS still needs verification at original timing.
- The [browser installer](https://bwdb07.github.io/afro-nx/installer/?v=3) creates an SD-ready ZIP with the tested NRO, the 30 FPS config, and shaders generated from your own European Xbox 360 disc.
